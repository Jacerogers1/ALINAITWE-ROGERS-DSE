About Us


JAMAR ICT Training Centre is a dedicated vocational and technology hub committed to bridging the digital divide by equipping students, professionals, and community members with essential computing and technical skills. Focused on practical, hands-on learning, the centre provides modern computer laboratories, reliable internet access, and structured courses ranging from basic digital literacy and office productivity to advanced networking, software applications, and hardware maintenance. By fostering an inclusive and supportive learning environment, the institution empowers individuals to navigate an increasingly technology-driven economy with confidence.


Beyond foundational instruction,Jamar ICT Training Centre emphasizes career readiness, innovation, and community empowerment. Through experienced instructors and industry-aligned curricula, learners gain real-world problem-solving abilities that enhance their employability and entrepreneurial potential. Whether preparing young people for their first roles in the tech sector or helping established professionals upgrade their digital competencies, the centre serves as a vital catalyst for personal growth and regional technological advancement
